# Zain's rules for every video (2026-11-07). Mandatory for all formats: nightly space shorts, sims, "you built it" series.

## Rule 1: triple hook in the first 1-2 seconds
All three land at once, from frame 0. No fade-in, no logo, no slow establishing shot.
- VISUAL HOOK: frame 0 is already the most striking image in the video, and it is moving (explosion, collapse, alert flashing, something mid-squish). Never open on a calm wide shot.
- VERBAL HOOK: narration starts within 0.3s. One line, under 8 words, present tense, a problem or a threat ("Your phone just screamed: take shelter.").
- TEXTUAL HOOK: big on-screen title from frame 0 that does NOT repeat the narration. It adds a second reason to stay (stakes, a number, or a question). Voice says "Tonight, the Sun disappears." Text says "8 MINUTES LEFT". Max 6 words.
The three must agree on one idea but each add something. If you can't explain the video from frame 0 alone, rewrite the opening.

## Rule 2: people share for reactions, not info
Nobody sends a video because it taught them something. They send it to get "BRO" or "no way" back. Every video needs at least one share moment:
- a jaw-drop beat: the twist, the reveal, the thing that should not work but does, placed at around 60-70% of the runtime and given a pause before it
- something to argue about: a choice or verdict viewers disagree on ("would you open the hatch?", "pick 1, 2 or 3")
- a "that's you" beat: a situation friends would tag each other in ("the friend who'd still be on their phone")
Cut lines that only explain. Keep a fact only if it makes the reaction bigger.
Endings ask for a reaction (pick a side, choose, guess), never "follow for more".

## Gate (check before publishing)
1. Look at frame 0: visual hook, text hook and speech all present? 2. Read the first line aloud: under 8 words, a threat or problem? 3. Name the share moment and its timestamp. If any of the three fails, fix it before rendering.
