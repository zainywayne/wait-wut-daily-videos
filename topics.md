## QUEUE (what ifs)
- [ ] What if Earth stopped spinning? (ground stops, air and oceans keep moving ~1,000 mph at the equator; huge winds and waves; day would last a year)
- [ ] What if the Sun became a black hole of the same mass? (Earth keeps orbiting; same gravity; but no light or heat, so it freezes; we'd see nothing change for 8 minutes)
- [ ] What if a Carrington-size solar storm hit today? (1859 storm fried telegraphs; today it could damage power grids and satellites; real risk scientists study)
- [ ] What if Earth lost its atmosphere? (unconscious in roughly 15 seconds, water boils at low pressure, sky goes black, temperature swings)
- [ ] What if a dinosaur-killer asteroid was heading for Earth tomorrow? (NASA DART 2022 hit a small asteroid and changed its orbit; we can only deflect with years of warning)
- [ ] What if all satellites stopped working at once? (GPS, weather, banking timing, phones, flights; about how much we depend on them)
- [ ] What if Earth's magnetic field vanished? (more radiation, auroras everywhere, satellite and grid trouble; atmosphere loss would be slow, over a very long time)
- [ ] What if the Moon was twice as close? (tides far bigger, Moon looks much larger; keep claims hedged)
- [ ] What if you fell into a black hole? (stretching from tidal forces, time slows as seen from outside; no one knows what's at the center)
- [ ] What if Saturn's rings disappeared? (rings are only about 10 m to 1 km thick in places; made of ice; they may be young and temporary)
- [ ] What if Earth had rings like Saturn? (bright ring across the sky; shadows on the ground; keep hedged)
- [ ] What if the Sun got 10 percent brighter? (oceans would eventually evaporate; runaway greenhouse risk; over a long time)
- [ ] What if you jumped out of the ISS without a suit? (about 400 km up, you're still orbiting at 28,000 km/h; you'd lose consciousness in seconds; no explosion myths)
- [ ] What if a neutron star passed through our solar system? (orbits thrown off; extreme gravity; planets could be flung away)
- [ ] What if the Moon crashed into Earth? (impact would melt the surface; the Moon likely formed from a collision billions of years ago, which is the leading theory)
- [ ] What if Mars replaced the Moon in our sky? (huge red disk; tides; keep hedged)
- [ ] What if a day lasted 48 hours? (longer heat and cold swings, sleep patterns, plants; days have been getting slightly longer over time)
- [ ] What if the ozone layer vanished? (more UV; sunburn in minutes; crop and ocean damage; the real hole was fixed by international action)
- [ ] What if Earth was suddenly 10 percent closer to the Sun? (much hotter; oceans warming; keep hedged)
- [ ] What if the ISS lost power and fell? (it would burn up and break apart; controlled deorbit is the plan; most pieces burn, some could reach the ocean)
